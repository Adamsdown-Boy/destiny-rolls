// Retraced Path - PvE Minor god AB 1
// (Fluted Barrel, Arrowhead Brake, Corkscrew Rifling, Polygonal Rifling, Smallbore, Hammer-Forged Rifling), (Tactical Battery, Light Battery, Enhanced Battery), (Subsistence), (One For All, Frenzy)
//notes:Constructed by @Adamsdown_Boy (PvE PvE-Minor PvE-SingleAdd PvE-EndGame PvE-God M+KB Controller): First-choice PvE roll for clearing individual Minor adds or tougher enemies in activities rated Normal, Hard or Very Hard difficulty. Retraced Path is an adaptive trace rifle, which are rated A-Tier for endgame PvE by @TheAegisRelic Endgame Analysis Spreadsheet​, who says "high utility for shot count-sensitive use cases, Harsh Refraction makes damage profile competitive". It's part of the Dare of Eternity loot pool, if you're looking for one, and is shapable if you unlock the pattern. The frame intrinsic offers built in Anti-Barrier, but offers no bonuses beyond being "well-rounded, reliable and sturdy" as per the Destiny Data Compendium. The Recoil Direction is pretty good at base, so you can tidy that up to 100 with the barrel perk, or look for your preference of Handling, Stability or Range. For the battery perk, Tactical or Light Battery boost gunfeel stats or Enhanced Battery adds a few extra shots. For endgame use against tougher Minor adds, in the first trait perk column to delay the need to reload, Subsistence reloads 20% of the battery on kills. In the second column for damage, One For All boosts damage by ×1.35 for a non-refreshable 11s after hitting three separate targets within 3s of each, or Frenzy after being in combat for 12s - that's dealing or taking damage - boosts Handling and Reload Speed by +100 and boosts damage by ×1.15 for a refreshable 7.2s, until you're out of combat again. Aegis ranks the Hot Swap origin trait as D-Tier for PvE - on a weapon kill you gain ×2 stacks up to ×6 max, you then use the stacks when readying or stowing for +100 Handling with a ×0.65 ready/stow animation scalar. If you're interested, the name is a double pun: signifying both a "Trace Rifle" and Bungie "retracing its steps" back Halo: Reach, modelling the weapon on the Covenant Focus Rifle for the Destiny 2 30th Anniversary Pack. Acasia's Dejection is an adaptive trace rifle, which are rated A-Tier for endgame PvE by @TheAegisRelic Endgame Analysis Spreadsheet​, who says "high utility for shot count-sensitive use cases, Harsh Refraction makes damage profile competitive". It's part of the Root of Nightmares loot pool, if you're looking for one, and is shapable if you unlock the pattern. The frame intrinsic offers built in Anti-Barrier, but offers no bonuses beyond being "well-rounded, reliable and sturdy" as per the Destiny Data Compendium. The Recoil Direction is pretty good at base, so you can tidy that up to 100 with the barrel perk, or look for your preference of Handling, Stability or Range. For the battery perk, Tactical or Light Battery boost gunfeel stats or Enhanced Battery adds a few extra shots. For double damage trait perks, you'll need a Solar subclass with a scorch ability to make the most of Burning Ambition in the first column to build Solar ignitions on tougher targets. If you can't apply scorch first, it'll take ~52 hits within 2s of each other to apply +20 scorch stacks, with +20 added on each additional hit afterwards to build towards an ignition. The scorch stacks of are increased by ×1.5 with Ember of Ashes. Alternatively in the first column, to delay the need to reload, Rewind Rounds refills 70% of hits landed from reserves when the battery empties, effectively increasing battery size by around ×3.0-3.3 if you hit all your shots, or Reconstruction refills 25% of the battery every 5.5s up to ×2 capacity when stowed or not firing. In the second column for damage, Paracausal Affinity boosts damage by ×1.2 for a refreshable 7s after any weapon or ability kill of a Light energy type. Aegis ranks the Harmonic Resonance origin trait as C-Tier for PvE - each additional Root of Nightmares weapon equipped adds an additional stack, each boosting Handling and Reload Speed, and applying damage boosts of ×1.033 for one or ×1.1 for two, against Dread and Lucent Hive Lightbearers. If you're interested, Acasia was a Psion whose partner was taken to be tortured as a sacrifice to Nezarec, Final God of Pain. She joined Nezarec's Acolytes in the desperate hope of freeing her lover, making "Dejection" reflect her profound grief and disillusionment.
